# ProductivAI Routine Archetypes & Scheduling Guidelines

Use these verified, sustainable routine templates when helping users structure their day (Workflow B and Workflow C). 

**Never design unrealistic schedules** (e.g. 10 hours of unbroken study without meals, exercise, or downtime). Always build in transitions and cognitive buffers.

---

## Archetype 1: The College Student (Classes + Evening Study)

*Designed for students balancing a 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM lecture schedule with exam preparation.*

- **Target Metrics**: Sleep: 7.5h | Study: 4.5h | Exercise: 30m | Passive Screen: 3h | Social Media: 1h | Breaks: 4 | Mood: 7–8
- **Predicted Score**: ~72 – 78 (High Productivity)

### Suggested Schedule Flow
- **07:00 – 08:30 AM**: Wake up, hydrate, breakfast, commute. *No morning doomscrolling.*
- **09:00 AM – 01:00 PM**: Morning lectures / lab sessions.
- **01:00 – 02:00 PM**: Lunch + social recovery (walk outside, relax).
- **02:00 – 04:00 PM**: Afternoon classes / project work.
- **04:30 – 05:30 PM**: **Physical Reset**: 30–45 min workout, sport, or brisk walk + shower.
- **06:00 – 07:30 PM**: **Deep Study Block 1** (90 mins): Hardest subject / high-priority assignment.
- **07:30 – 08:30 PM**: Dinner and mental pause.
- **08:30 – 10:00 PM**: **Deep Study Block 2** (90 mins): Problem sets, revision, or review notes.
- **10:00 – 11:00 PM**: Wind-down, low-stimulation reading, personal time.
- **11:00 PM**: Sleep.

## Archetype 2: The Deep Work Engineer / Remote Knowledge Worker

*Designed for software engineers, writers, and knowledge professionals who require uninterrupted maker time.*

- **Target Metrics**: Sleep: 8h | Work/Study: 6.5h | Exercise: 40m | Passive Screen: 3.5h | Social Media: 0.5h | Breaks: 5 | Mood: 8
- **Predicted Score**: ~80 – 85 (High Productivity)

### Suggested Schedule Flow
- **07:30 – 08:30 AM**: Sunlight exposure, light stretching/movement, coffee/breakfast.
- **08:45 – 11:15 AM**: **Deep Work Sprint 1** (150 min block: 50m work / 10m break / 50m work / 10m break / 30m review). Complex coding / core feature development.
- **11:30 AM – 12:30 PM**: Communications, team async replies, PR reviews.
- **12:30 – 01:30 PM**: Lunch away from the desk + brief outdoor walk.
- **01:30 – 03:30 PM**: **Deep Work Sprint 2** (120 mins): System design, bug fixes, focused implementation.
- **03:30 – 04:15 PM**: Standups, meetings, or admin wrap-up.
- **04:30 – 05:30 PM**: Gym / run / physical training.
- **Evening**: Relax, hobbies, disconnect completely from work notifications.

## Archetype 3: The Exam Crunch Sprint (2–3 Weeks Out)

*High cognitive demand, sustainable only for limited periods. Preserves sleep to protect retention.*

- **Target Metrics**: Sleep: 7.5h | Study: 8h | Exercise: 25m | Passive Screen: 2h | Social Media: 0.5h | Breaks: 6 | Mood: 7
- **Predicted Score**: ~84 – 89 (High Productivity)

### Core Rules for Exam Sprints
1. **Sleep is Non-Negotiable**: Pulling all-nighters reduces memory consolidation by up to 40%. Keep sleep $\ge 7\text{h}$.
2. **Active Recall over Passive Reading**: Use Feynman technique, past papers, flashcards, and practice problems.
3. **Pomodoro Cycling (50/10)**: 50 minutes high-intensity focus, 10 minutes physical movement away from screens.
4. **App Blocker Mandate**: Put phone in another room or activate strict grayscale/app limits during study blocks.

## Archetype 4: The Recovery / Momentum Reset

*Designed for users experiencing burnout, severe procrastination, or post-exam mental exhaustion.*

- **Target Metrics**: Sleep: 9h | Study: 2h | Exercise: 45m | Passive Screen: 4h | Social Media: 1.5h | Breaks: 3 | Mood: 6
- **Predicted Score**: ~58 – 65 (Moderate Productivity)

### Principles for Recovery
- Lower the friction threshold: 1 dedicated 45-minute focus session is a massive win.
- Prioritize restorative sleep and physical movement to rebalance neurotransmitters.
- Eliminate self-blame: Focus on building 1 solid habit today rather than solving the entire week.
